# Wolfpack Labs SEO Update

Updated July 23, 2026.

## Main changes

- Repositioned the homepage from a legal-document hub to a product-focused Swift Invoice landing page.
- Targeted the primary audience and search intent: contractors, freelancers, self-employed professionals, and small service businesses.
- Added three substantial, internally linked pages:
  - `invoice-app-for-contractors.html`
  - `invoice-app-for-freelancers.html`
  - `invoice-vs-estimate.html`
- Rewrote page titles, meta descriptions, social metadata, headings, navigation, and internal anchor text.
- Added Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, and Article structured data where relevant.
- Corrected the sitemap's future-dated homepage entry and added the new pages plus image metadata.
- Improved crawl directives and preserved `noindex` on payment and estimate-response utility pages.
- Removed duplicate homepage drafts (`index-.html` and `index2.html`).
- Removed an unused 27 MB duplicate video file.
- Reduced screenshot image payload from roughly 11 MB to roughly 1.3 MB while retaining high-resolution zoom images.
- Replaced the oversized favicon references with correctly sized icon files.
- Disabled homepage video autoplay and changed the video to `preload="none"` to avoid unnecessary initial downloads.
- Rebuilt the social-sharing image around Swift Invoice and its intended audiences.
- Added a GitHub Pages `CNAME`, web manifest, and post-deployment search-engine checklist.

## Important

These changes improve crawlability, relevance, search-result presentation, internal linking, and page performance. Search engines still decide whether and where pages rank. Organic growth will also depend on indexing, useful first-hand content, external mentions/links, product demand, and user engagement after deployment.
