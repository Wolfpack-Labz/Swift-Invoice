Common questions

About WP Labs: Invoice

Who is WP Labs: Invoice designed for?

WP Labs: Invoice is designed for contractors, freelancers, self-employed professionals, and small service businesses that need a simpler way to create invoices and estimates, track payment status, and organize customer work.

Can I create both estimates and invoices?

Yes. The app includes workflows for preparing estimates, recording customer responses, creating invoices, and keeping related customer and job details organized.

Can I attach job photos?

Yes. Job images can be connected to invoices, estimates, and customer emails, helping service providers preserve visual context and document completed work.

Is WP Labs: Invoice available now?

Yes. WP Labs: Invoice is live on the Apple App Store and available for iPhone users to download now. You can also scan the QR code on this page to open the App Store listing directly on your device.
