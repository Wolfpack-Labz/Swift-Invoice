Common questions

About Swift Invoice

Who is Swift Invoice designed for?

Swift Invoice is designed for contractors, freelancers, self-employed professionals, and small service businesses that need a simpler way to create invoices and estimates, track payment status, and organize customer work.

Can I create both estimates and invoices?

Yes. The app includes workflows for preparing estimates, recording customer responses, creating invoices, and keeping related customer and job details organized.

Can I attach job photos?

Yes. Job images can be connected to invoices, estimates, and customer emails, helping service providers preserve visual context and document completed work.

When will Swift Invoice be available?

The public launch is planned for Fall 2026. Join the launch update list for App Store availability and release announcements.
