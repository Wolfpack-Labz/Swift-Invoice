Built for field-to-customer work

Useful when the office is wherever the job happens

Independent tradespeople and service providers often gather information at a customer location, then recreate it later in another tool. Swift Invoice is designed to reduce that duplication by keeping estimates, invoices, customers, job photos, and payment information together.

  • Reuse saved items and services instead of typing common work repeatedly.

Contractor invoicing checklist

What a contractor invoice should clearly show

A professional invoice should make it easy for the customer to understand who performed the work, what was provided, what is owed, and how to respond.

Business and customer information

Include your business identity, customer name, contact information, and the correct service or billing details.

Detailed labor and materials

Use clear line items with descriptions, quantities, rates, and amounts so customers can connect the bill to the completed work.

Dates, totals, and payment terms

Show the invoice date, due information, subtotal, taxes or discounts when applicable, and the final balance.

Supporting notes or photos

Add relevant notes and job documentation when they help explain the scope, completion, or customer expectations.

Frequently asked questions

Contractor invoice app questions

Can contractors create estimates before invoicing?

Yes. Swift Invoice supports estimate workflows before the final invoice, helping contractors organize pricing and customer responses earlier in the job.

Can I attach photos to a contractor invoice?

Yes. Job photos can be connected to invoices, estimates, and customer communications so visual documentation remains with the relevant work.

Is Swift Invoice full accounting software?

Swift Invoice is focused on practical invoicing, estimating, customer, job, and payment workflows. It is intended to be simpler than a full enterprise accounting platform.
