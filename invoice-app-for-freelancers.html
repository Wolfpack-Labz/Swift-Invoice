Frequently asked questions

Freelancer invoice app questions

Can I customize a freelance invoice?

Swift Invoice includes business branding and document customization workflows so client-facing invoices and estimates can reflect your business identity.

Can I save repeat freelance services?

Yes. Saved items and templates help reduce repeated entry for common services, products, rates, and descriptions.

Can I send an estimate before an invoice?

Yes. You can prepare estimates for client review and keep the estimate workflow connected to later invoicing.
